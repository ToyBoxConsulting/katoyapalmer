    Five Windows Into a Fragile Economy

    Where the series begins. I share my own road, from raising my son as a single mom on SSI and Section 8 to running operations as a COO, and use it as a lens on how fragile our safety nets really are. Five windows follow: housing, community safety, nonprofits, technology and inclusive growth, all making one case. Inclusion is not charity. It is infrastructure.

  2. Mindfulness in the Middle · 3 min read

    Reclaiming Myself: How Mindfulness Helped Me Rebuild From Rock Bottom

    Where Mindfulness in the Middle started. After a year that took jobs, titles and routines, I went back to the practice that first steadied me in therapy as a single mom, and to a card deck that had sat on my shelf for years. One card a week, lived with and shared honestly. This is about practice, not expertise.

    Somebody Handed Me a Chair

    I moved to Bellevue sure I would have to decide which parts of myself to bring. Then three boards, each opened by a person, made room for me: somebody handed me a chair, not a guest pass. This is about that welcome, how I passed it on with Golden Hour Unboxed, and why you should bring all of yourself into the room.
