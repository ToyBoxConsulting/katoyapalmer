Mindfulness in the Middle has a home here now.

It began as my healing journey, a path to finding myself again. Small practices for staying whole in the thick of it: the middle of change, the middle of recovery, the middle of trying again.

Start with one breath Read the essays
The practice
“This isn't about expertise. I'm not a guru. It's about practice.”
Katoya Palmer, July 2025
What it is

The space I come back to when I need to anchor.

I started Mindfulness in the Middle on TikTok in the summer of 2025. I needed something simple and steady to hold onto. A deck of mindfulness cards gave me the structure: one card, one theme, one reflection.

Somewhere along the way, I wasn't just following the cards anymore. I was journaling in public.

The middle isn't just middle age. It's the middle of change, the middle of recovery, the middle of uncertainty, the middle of trying again. It's about care before crisis, rest as resistance, and remembering that authenticity matters more than performance.

Mindfulness in the Middle was my healing journey, a path to finding myself again. That same path is what inspired Lipstick on My Fork and SZND by Katoya.

Last year I said I didn't know if this series would end at 10 episodes or 100. What I did know is that I was still here. I still am, and so is the practice. Now it has a home here, and one practice lands in my weekly letter every Monday.

The practice

One full breath.

It's what I do before every speaking engagement or presentation.

4
In
4
Hold
6
Out

In for four, hold for four, out for six. One round resets more than you'd think.

More small practices.

Sixty seconds, no cushion required. Grounding you can do at your desk, in the car, mid-shift.

01

Name five things

Five you can see, four you can touch. Pull yourself back into the room you're in.

02

The honest pause

Before you answer, react, or send, one beat to choose instead of just respond.

03

Put it down

Name the one worry you're carrying that isn't yours to hold today. Set it down.

04

Hand on heart

A palm to the chest, three breaths. Steadiness you can give yourself, anywhere.

05

Light the candle

One small ritual to mark the shift from doing to being. Begin and end on purpose.

06

Ask for help

Notice one place you're carrying more than you need to. Ask: what would shift if I let someone help me?
