Sunday, October 25, 2026

Ascend Prime Steak & Sushi, Bellevue

💋💋💋💋💋 I'd skip home-cooking for a month.

I've celebrated a lot of moments here, 31 floors up. June 2022 was the celebration after the premiere of No More Under's documentary Drowning in Silence, executive produced by Hill Harper and directed by Chezik Tsunoda. Hill passed the basket, Dr. Linda Quan (UW professor emerita and drowning-prevention researcher) was at the table, and I took the quail egg shot that used to be on the menu.

October 2022 was a night out with the lights low and The 1921, an Ascend classic served smoking. June 2023 was a solo lunch with a lake view before meeting friends at Julia's on Broadway. And every year, a board I serve on holds its annual lunch here. This year, the deviled eggs made the buffet.

My standing order

The Nitamago deviled eggs: soy-poached egg, bacon-pea jam and braised pork belly, still on the happy hour menu.

Trivia

Ascend has a six-tier steak menu, from Creekstone Prime all the way to Miyazaki A5 Wagyu. And that purple swirl is Okinawan sweet potato, ube's purple cousin.

The Dedication

This one is dedicated to the chefs on site.

Would I go back?

There have been more visits than the camera caught, including a celebration dinner for a former staff member who's now a colleague. I'll keep celebrating here.

Sunday, October 18, 2026

Hilltop Red Apple, Beacon Hill

💋💋💋💋 Swoon. Bring a date worth it.

If you know, you know. The fried catfish in the hot case at Hilltop Red Apple is the reason I once drove all the way to Beacon Hill for a fix, and this week I almost did it again.

It's a little nostalgic too. Before Beacon Hill, there was the Red Apple that used to be on Jackson. My mom and I stopped there for catfish on so many Sundays after church.

Flavor

The fish is usually fresh, and it's fried the way I like it. Nothing tastes like home-cooked catfish to me yet, but this is my fix until it does. The hushpuppies are a must, and this visit I got a little peach cobbler too.

Service

The staff is always super friendly.

Lipstick Test

None left. Pass the tartar sauce and hot sauce.

The Dedication

This one goes out to the Hilltop staff. Always super friendly.

Would I go back?

I almost did tonight.

Sunday, October 11, 2026

Primo's, Bellevue

💋💋💋💋 Swoon. Bring a date worth it.

I've been coming back since January 2026. The first visit I worked through most of the appetizers with a colleague and fell for the drinks, the potato salad and the ribeye. Then I found Taco Tuesday: the tacos and sides are worth the trip, and my taco plates came with a free drink. I've also sampled their seasonal pasta and gnocchi at events, and they hosted my taste test for Golden Hour Unboxed.

The chicken and waffles took me back to my grandma's fried chicken. And the house salad is my must, every single time. It comes with steak, wrapped like a bouquet, and it's the best deal on the menu.

The vibe

Live music most times I've been, and a playlist that jams when there isn't. The staff is always friendly and professional. My move is to go right at opening, when it's a little quieter.

The tab

Prices are decent if you have discipline. Drinks will bump the tab up quickly, so plan accordingly, or go on Taco Tuesday.

Lipstick Test

Wiped clean. Every time.

The Dedication

This one goes out to Chuck. I didn't know him before Golden Hour Unboxed. Primo's was the first restaurant to answer, and he didn't hesitate. He made the evening. That's what showing up for community looks like.

Kiss & Tell 💋

Primo's is named for the owners' friend Juan "Primo." Chuck also runs Vue in Belltown, karaoke suites and all.

Would I go back?

I can't wait to.
