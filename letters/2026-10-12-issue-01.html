No better time than today

Welcome to the first issue. Every Monday, this letter rounds up everything I shared that week, across my consulting work, my writing and my kitchen, plus the places I showed up and the causes I'm supporting. One letter, so you don't have to follow me everywhere.

Why now? Because there's no better time than today. Those close to me have heard my newest mantra: "I'm tired of losing." I realized I was only losing because I stopped competing. Not that this is a competition, but I do my best when I have a goal and something to strive for. I've dedicated my time to building my business, and this letter is how I'll bring it all together.

Where I was: 4Culture's Network of Support mixer

Wednesday night I was at 4Culture's Network of Support mixer at El Centro de la Raza's Centilia Cultural Center, my first year as one of the program's consultants. Over three rounds of 10-minute conversations, people asked what a board is really for, how to grow the next layer of leaders, and where to start on a strategic plan, a succession plan or a grant. A warm, welcoming room.

Mindfulness in the Middle
