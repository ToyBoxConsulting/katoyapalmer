Thought

Prevention is infrastructure

I never lost a life on my watch at the pool. In community safety work, I saw dozens lost. The difference wasn't courage. It was infrastructure.

That's the thread through this week's posts: every safe kid I've known had an adult and a place to be, and people never asked me for a unit. They asked for a home near the bus, the school and the job. I heard that in the affordable housing strategy sessions I facilitated in 2025, and this year the plan was adopted.

It's also why I was at Ripples of Hope last month, No More Under's benefit, celebrating seven years of saving lives. Who learns to swim in this country was shaped by who was allowed in the water. That was designed, so it can be redesigned. Drowning in Silence, the film I'm in, is streaming on Apple TV. Watch it, share it, and book the swim lessons.

Taste

SZND: four years of basil

My first real harvest went into a big pan of penne pesto for my water polo team in 2021.

Summer 2025 was my best basil year ever, and it started with sick basil starts I bought at 50 percent off. Four weeks later the plants were bigger than the planter, and that harvest became my Cajun pesto: steak and pesto pasta, garden to table. If you've never grown anything, start with basil starts, even the ones on the clearance rack. What are you growing? Hit reply.

Honorable mention for football season: deviled eggs. 🏈 Game day calls for them. Last Thanksgiving my son made his first batch and carried the tray to the table himself, and shout-out to my aunt, uncle and cousins in Arizona: I have to have their deviled eggs every time I go. How do you top yours?

If you were graded in cursive

At Comic-Con, my son and I picked out new pens at the same table, and we were taught to use them completely differently.

I learned to type on my mom's typewriter, her college graduation gift. Now I talk to my phone and it types for me, and AI fixes my sentence before I finish it. Shout-out to my mom and her red pen. What were you taught was non-negotiable that doesn't matter anymore? Hit reply.

Our Husky journey

Update: my son was granted a full year of tuition, loan-free, through UW's undergraduate grant program. Won't He do it?! 🤯👏

Transfer students start behind on financial aid, and we found out the hard way. I'm so thankful my kiddo gets to bear the fruit of his labor through such an amazing gesture. If you're a transfer family still working through aid, hit reply. I'll share what we learned.

Mindfulness in the Middle: this week's sit

A guided visualization: a picture, slow enough that your mind settles into it.

Picture a cup of tea you made recently. Watch the steam rise and thin out as it meets the air. Feel the warmth through the cup in your hands. Now watch the steam slow and the surface go still. Take a deep breath in. And a deep breath out.

Off the clock

Onyx called it. 🐾 The other night, little Onyx was so uneasy and clingy that I said out loud, "I hope we're not about to have an earthquake." He didn't settle down until way after 2 a.m. Then there was an earthquake near us in Snoqualmie. Do your pets ever know something before you do?

Two Seahawks losses in one week. Damn it, Sam! 💙💚 The interceptions got me, and I could not believe we went into overtime. I don't know the most about football, but I'm fairly athletic, and the offensive line has some work to do. It also makes me wonder: does the quarterback who wins us a Super Bowl get some kind of curse that keeps them from bringing home another one? Still riding with them.

Monopoly is back at McDonald's, and I've been playing since high school. What grinds my gears: last year you could get free plays online, and this year the only free way in is by mail. Still playing. The game runs through November 1, and you can enter codes in the app until November 22. What's your strategy?
