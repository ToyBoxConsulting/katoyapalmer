Cover story

01

You can do this

Thanksgiving is won on Wednesday.

Gobble gobble. It's time to prep. It's official: Mom asked me to cook Thanksgiving dinner this year, so the kitchen is mine. Five of us plus leftovers, dinner at 3. She likes traditional, I like a little razzle dazzle, and this year her peach cobbler is on my table for the first time.

That's my baked mac and cheese on the cover, the one with the crust. Mac and cheese is big in my culture. It is my son's favorite. It is my mom's favorite. It is my favorite. Mom prefers a traditional Southern casserole style. I like a little razzle dazzle, a rosemary asiago and some other seasoning in mine.

In her voice

Mac and cheese is just one of the most controversial yet most looked to items on a Thanksgiving table in my home and in my family. Mom prefers a traditional Southern casserole style mac and cheese. I like a little razzle dazzle, like to add a rosemary asiago and some other types of seasoning into mine. And of course, lots of milk or half and half or a cream, just to get that macaroni nice and smooth. What I've learned over the years is that the old school way of putting the butter in on top of the noodles and then the seasoning isn't actually right, although that's the mac and cheese I've had my whole life and I've loved. I've stopped putting the butter in directly and started adding it after the cheese has gone in.

Anything that can be made ahead is done by Wednesday night. Thursday is only the hot food.

Preference isn't capacity

A style profile tells you what you reach for first. It doesn't tell you what you can do when the room needs it.

I took a personality test this fall. It said I don't lead with influence. Fair. I don't prefer it. But I naturally do it, and that's the part no test can see. What do you do well that your profile says isn't your style?

Presence

05

The kitchen is mine

The greens come from Mom on Saturday, with the turkey.

She picks them up from Carpinito Brothers in Kent. I like to mix the collards with a bit of mustard and turnip, the southern trio, and we'll see what I can find this year. Sometimes I can only find mustards to add to the collards. Sometimes I can find all three. Ham hocks on Thanksgiving, smoked turkey the rest of the year.

Mindfulness in the Middle

This week's sit

Happy harvest season from my kitchen.

Happy harvest season from my kitchen. This time of year is a reminder of my blessings and how my family's been blessed. Anytime we're able to put together a traditional Thanksgiving, we know we've been blessed in that year. Before the table fills: one hand on the counter. In for four. Hold for four. Out for six. Then back to it.

Off the clock

07

Present and accounted for by 8 AM

I have to be present and accounted for by 8:00 AM for the next two days.

How are my fellow geriatric millennials factoring in their GTA game time with all your responsibilities?

Yes, I got it, compliments of my son, who couldn't live without it until I was ready to get it. We share an Xbox account and I got lucky. It took 8 to 9 hours to download, so that was a buzzkill. I got through the first mission with his help, although I didn't ask, because he was still waiting on his download. It's fun. I like the changes so far, and the media is interesting to see. I'm looking forward to getting further in and learning what 13 years of change did for GTA.
