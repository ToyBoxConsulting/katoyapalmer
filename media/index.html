Featured Appearance

"I take nothing and make it into something."

On All My Friends Are Dope with Eric Calligraphy · Converge Media / Black Media Matters

Katoya sat down with her best friend Eric for a long-form conversation — the kind you only get after a decade of showing up for each other. From growing up in Louisiana, to moving to the Muckleshoot Indian Reservation at 8, to founding ToyBox Consulting — this is the wide-frame version of the story.

They cover: what she actually does as a fractional COO ("I take nothing and make it into something"), why she calls herself a composer of experiences, the corporate harm that pushed her toward mindfulness, the moment she stopped letting other people set her goals, Cameron heading to UW, and the difference between a shell and a butterfly's wings.

Also: the Golden Hour event announcement, the Gucci Mane book recommendation that surprised no one, and one piece of free game — stay sucker free.
