01 · Cover story
You can do this

Thanksgiving is won on Wednesday.

Gobble gobble. It's time to prep. It's official: Mom asked me to cook Thanksgiving dinner this year, so the kitchen is mine. Five of us plus leftovers, dinner at 3. She likes traditional, I like a little razzle dazzle, and this year her peach cobbler is on my table for the first time.

That's my baked mac and cheese on the cover, the one with the crust. Mac and cheese is big in my culture. It is my son's favorite. It is my mom's favorite. It is my favorite. Mom prefers a traditional Southern casserole style. I like a little razzle dazzle, a rosemary asiago and some other seasoning in mine.

My blueprint: anything that can be made ahead is done by Wednesday night. Thursday is only the hot food.

Cranberry sauce was first off the list. Fifteen minutes on the stove. That is the whole job. The recipe →

Saturday the whole blueprint went up as a reel, day by day, in my voice. Sunday, cranberry sauce start to finish, then Monday's cornbread and the broth, because Monday is cornbread day. Not for eating. For the dressing.

02 · Thought
Preference isn't capacity

A style profile tells you what you reach for first. It doesn't tell you what you can do when the room needs it.

I took a personality test this fall. It said I don't lead with influence. Fair. I don't prefer it. But I naturally do it, and that's the part no test can see. What do you do well that your profile says isn't your style?

Where are the parents. I asked it in July, after the Space Needle shooting. Not to blame. Every safe kid I've known had an adult and a place to be. Prevention is not a moment. It is a system we keep choosing to fund, or not. Read the essay →

03 · Network of Support
A big hello to the new readers

This has been an amazing week for my Network of Support work with 4Culture grantees.

If you found me through 4Culture's Network of Support, welcome. This week I picked up my first project prepping a workshop series, onboarded a new client, and signed a new organization. A big hello to all of you.

What a letter of agreement with me covers, in plain terms:

•A kickoff and a working plan, so we both know what we are building and in what order.
•Your board: a board survey and gap analysis, a Board 101 leave-behind, a full-day retreat or one working session at your own board meeting, and coaching for your officers.
•Your money: grant budgets and restricted-fund setup, so each grant has its own numbers and its own report, plus internal controls and a finance policies manual.
•Your fundraising: an annual fundraiser plan with a timeline and a run of show, and support on the event itself.
•Between sessions: items you can act on without me, working sessions when you need them, and a close-out so the work stays with your team.

Hours are estimates, billed as used, and 4Culture covers them for the organizations it prioritizes. You always work with me directly.

04 · Presence
The kitchen is mine

The greens come from Mom on Saturday, with the turkey. She picks them up from Carpinito Brothers in Kent. I like to mix the collards with a bit of mustard and turnip, the southern trio, and we'll see what I can find this year. Ham hocks on Thanksgiving, smoked turkey the rest of the year.

This week's sit. Happy harvest season from my kitchen. This time of year is a reminder of my blessings and how my family's been blessed. Anytime we're able to put together a traditional Thanksgiving, we know we've been blessed in that year. Before the table fills: one hand on the counter. In for four. Hold for four. Out for six. Then back to it. Mindfulness in the Middle →
